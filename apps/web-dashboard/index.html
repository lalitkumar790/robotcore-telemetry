Research pipeline

The order of work is designed to protect reproducibility
MILESTONE 01

Protocol verification

Golden packet tests, CRC, sequencing, and transmitter/receiver compatibility.

MILESTONE 02

Data quality

Time semantics, GPS validity, sequence gaps, radio settings, and provenance.

MILESTONE 03

Measured coverage

Map observed tracks, RSSI/SNR, and packet delivery with quality flags.

MILESTONE 04

Prediction & planning

Evaluate spatial models and route objectives against explicit baselines.
