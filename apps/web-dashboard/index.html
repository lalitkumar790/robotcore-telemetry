Research pipeline

The order of work protects reproducibility and prevents receiver gaps from being mistaken for a continuous path.
MILESTONE 01

Device & protocol health

Identify permitted serial/BLE devices and verify RTP version, CRC and sequence.

MILESTONE 02

Dual-ended capture

Keep Pi .rcm samples and laptop receiver CSV separate, immutable and session-linked.

MILESTONE 03

Measured coverage

Show received/lost sample positions, RSSI/SNR, PDR and visible data gaps.

MILESTONE 04

Prediction & planning

Only after validated field logs: evaluate link prediction and communication-aware route choices.

Hardware & data feeds

Device status

Only authorized browser ports and devices are inspected. Hardware is shown as connected only after a real connection or valid telemetry is observed.

Connection model: this HTTPS dashboard can read a user-selected LoRa-E5 USB serial receiver, a user-selected GPS serial stream, or the WT901BLE68 BLE characteristic. It cannot silently enumerate or control unapproved hardware. The Pi's onboard mission recorder continues independently of LoRa and is imported as a .rcm file for loss reconstruction.
